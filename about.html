About

My name is Chloe Prock, and I am currently pursuring my PhD in Interdiscplinary Design and Media and Northeastern University, in the College of Arts, Media, and Design. I graduated Summa Cum Laude from Northeastern University with a BFA in Graphic and Information Design, and an MS in Information Design and Data Visualization.

I am a member of the Khoury College of Computer Science Data Visualization Research Lab, where I am working on research related to Visualization Ethics, with a particular focus on statistical visualization and science communication. More broadly, my research explores the intersections of metaphor, experience, feminism, and emotion in data visualization design, where I take a practice-based approach informed by my work on climate-focused data visualization.
